## Prioritized implementation plan

1. **P0 — Unify booking and money invariants.** Make one pricing/snapshot contract apply to every enabled booking path. Implement approved slab boundaries, additional-weight, surcharge and GST rules. Validate the selected mode with server-calculated carrier-specific weight. Add database-level AWB assignment protection appropriate to verified identifiers, and durable idempotency/reconciliation for covered allocation paths. Test legacy records, overlap, boundary values, timeouts and concurrency.
2. **P0 — Establish the configuration and operations baseline.** Obtain approved pricing, billing, GST and finance inputs. Approve and test the RBAC policy and role assignments, confirm transaction-capable MongoDB, and establish backup and restore evidence. In parallel, obtain the three external contracts identified below, confirm provider status mappings, and identify the next real courier partner.
3. **P1 — Finish the booking and pickup operator workflows.** Put editable drafts and unresolved bookings into the appropriate Pending AWB views. Add an owned label action after AWB confirmation. Add package category and special handling, align origin address with the registered site when applicable, and implement Surface/Express comparison and available quote metadata. Preserve full pickup data across local and carrier scheduling, then complete pickup lifecycle.
4. **P1 — Finish tracking and dashboard.** Add scheduled fallback polling and operational notifications. Complete SRD status normalization, NDR/RTO lifecycle and dashboard metric-to-list parity. Implement contract-dependent features only after validating the approved wire contract locally and in an isolated staging account.
5. **P1 — Complete settlement and reports.** Unify credit outstanding, add customer statements, configurable billing periods, adjustments and shipment-linked walk-in receipts. Finish monetary profitability per customer, margin percentage, daily/weekly profit, report filter/export parity and required historical records.
6. **P2 — Implement advanced scope once the underlying records are reliable.** Build partner performance reporting and ranking, complete the SRD-defined advanced analytics workstream with the agreed metrics, and address Phase 4 automated selection and further partner additions. Keep manual selection as the initial behavior. Add integrated browser-to-API verification against isolated MongoDB and fixture-backed adapters for the highest-risk workflows.
